      WELCOME TO THE CLEARING

      Make a little room for joy.

      Each friend brings a plate wish and a skewer wish. Grant both to light a lantern and welcome the next friend, a little later in the evening.

      1. Merge on the cloth. Drag matching fruit together. Place a third between two, or a fourth inside a triangle. Gold rings show every fruit that will join, and bigger center combos hold a little surprise.
      2. Plan a chain. A new fruit that touches its twin merges again. The preview card shows the final result before you let go.
      3. Serve and thread. Drop requested fruit on the plate. Thread three of the pictured fruit onto the wooden skewer. Anything else shared on the plate simply makes room.
      4. The basket keeps giving. Every three merges, servings or threadings, fresh fruit arrives by itself. Tapping the basket brings fruit right away and counts as an action. Rearranging is always free.
      5. Listen to your guests. Some friends are shy or love a riddle. A wish marked “?” is still a secret: guess it, and they’ll be delighted.
      6. Stars, if you like. Finish at or under par for three stars and find one of Grandma Hazel’s recipe cards. There are no timers and no way to lose.

      Keyboard: N selects · arrows move · Enter places · T serves · K threads · B basket · U undo · Esc cancels.
